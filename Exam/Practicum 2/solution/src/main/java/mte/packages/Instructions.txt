You run a package company.  Each package has 3 characteristics: Priorty, Length, and
Weight.  A Package class and Priority enum has been provided.  For each part, make the
necessary updates to support the functionality.  Updates may include updating the Package
class, updating the Priority enum, or adding new types (classes, interfaces, enums).

Priority sort order is always High, Medium, and then Low.

Part 1
In the factory, packages are sorted according to the Natural Order of Priority.  The
Length and Weight do not matter. After making your changes, test that your that
packages are sorted correctly by uncommenting and updating the Collections.sort() method
in main for Part 1.

Part 2
When loading onto a truck, packages are sorted according to the Priority and then by
Length (ascending).  After making your changes, test that your that packages are sorted
correctly by uncommenting and updating the Collections.sort() method in main for Part 2.

Part 3
When loading onto a plane, packages are sorted according to the Priority and then by
Weight (descending).  After making your changes, test that your that packages are sorted
correctly by uncommenting and updating the Collections.sort() method in main for Part 3.
